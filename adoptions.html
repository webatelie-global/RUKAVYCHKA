Happy Endings

Every rescue story deserves a happy ending. These are the dogs who found their families, their safe places and the love they had been waiting for.

From rescue to forever home

Behind every happy ending is a journey. Some of these dogs came from difficult situations, but today they have warm beds, loving families and a future full of happiness.

Fedya

🇭🇺 Budapest
About 5 years old

Fedya was rescued from a terrible boarding facility, where he had spent six months in a tiny cage. He still had stitches from his neutering surgery and a large pressure sore on his chest because he had barely been able to move.

Today, Fedya lives in Budapest with his family, where he is the most loved boy in the world. He has his own sofa, sleeps in his mum's bed under a warm blanket and enjoys trips to the forest.

His very own home, and all the love he deserves. 🩵

Lenya

🇦🇹 Vienna
About 5–6 years old

Our sweet Lenya travelled to Vienna to join his sister Nelly. They are now the best of friends and finally get to enjoy life together.

Lenya now goes to grooming appointments, enjoys good-quality food that does not trigger his allergies, explores the streets of Vienna and spends cosy evenings on the sofa with his mum and dad.

Together again, safe and loved. ❤️

Bonya

🇦🇹 Vienna
About 7 years old

Bonya was rescued by soldiers in Kramatorsk. She had been through so much: her tail had been shot off, she had serious dental problems that made eating difficult, and she also needed surgery to remove a benign lipoma. We raised funds to help pay for her treatment.

After everything she endured, Bonya finally found her way to Vienna and into a loving, responsible family. Now she enjoys walks in her warm little jacket, sleeps on the sofa wrapped in a blanket and receives all the health check-ups she needs.

A new life filled with care, comfort and love. ❤️
