Everyday life at RUKAVYCHKA

A look behind the scenes: the daily care, the happy moments, the hard work and the love that keeps this shelter going.

A lot of hungry mouths

Every day begins with preparing food. Large pots of porridge and meals are made for the dogs. Feeding so many animals takes time, energy and a lot of ingredients.

For the dogs, it is one of the happiest moments of the day. For the people caring for them, it is a responsibility that never takes a day off.

[PHOTO: Valentin cooking porridge or preparing dog food]
[PHOTO: Dogs eating their meals]

Mealtime happiness

A full bowl means comfort, energy and another day with a little less worry. Every dog deserves regular meals and enough food.

Care, every single day

Feeding is only one part of shelter life. There is cleaning, checking on the dogs, looking after their health, giving medicines when needed and making sure everyone is safe.

Some dogs need more attention than others. Each has a different personality, history and set of needs.

The moments that make it all worthwhile

Not every moment is difficult. There are games, funny expressions, wagging tails, naps in the sunshine and friendships between dogs.

Preparing for difficult days

The war has made shelter life more difficult. Damaged areas, cold weather and uncertainty mean there is always something to repair, prepare or improve.

Keeping the dogs warm and protected takes planning, materials, fuel and helping hands.
