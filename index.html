Who we are

A place where every life matters.

RUKAVYCHKA is a small animal shelter in Ukraine, built on kindness, patience and love for animals.

Every day means feeding, cleaning, caring, treating and making sure that our four-legged friends are safe. It is not always easy, but every wagging tail reminds us why we keep going.

Every animal deserves care, dignity and a chance.
