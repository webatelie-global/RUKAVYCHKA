Every helping hand matters

You can help RUKAVYCHKA in many ways. A little support, given with kindness, can make a big difference to a dog's life.

What the dogs need

Caring for a shelter means meeting everyday needs, whatever the weather or circumstances. Here are some of the most important ways to help.

🥣

Food & nutrition

Food, grains and ingredients for preparing daily meals. A full bowl is one of the most basic ways to show care.

💊

Medical care

Medicines, vaccinations, parasite prevention, veterinary visits and treatment for dogs who need extra care.

🪵

Warmth & winter

Firewood, insulation, repairs and materials to help keep the dogs warm and protected during cold months.

⛽

Fuel & electricity

Fuel and diesel for essential journeys and the generator, especially when electricity is unavailable.

🏠

Shelter repairs

Materials and practical help to repair and improve kennels and make the shelter safer.

❤️

Time & kindness

Volunteering, sharing our story, helping with transport or simply telling someone about the dogs.

Small help. Real difference.

A bag of food, a box of medicine, a share on social media or a regular donation — every contribution helps us care for the dogs who are still waiting for a family.
