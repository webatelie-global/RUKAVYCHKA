A story of care, courage and hope

Behind every rescued dog is a story. Behind RUKAVYCHKA are two people who chose to make a difference.

It started with compassion

RUKAVYCHKA began on the property of Valentin and Vera, long before the full-scale war changed life in Ukraine. They opened their hearts and their home to dogs who had nowhere else to go.

What began as a personal act of kindness gradually became a shelter — a place where abandoned and vulnerable dogs could find food, safety and care.

Valentin and Vera

Valentin is over 70, but his days are still shaped by the needs of the dogs. He often starts around five in the morning and continues until late at night.

He prepares food, feeds the dogs, cleans, checks on them and takes care of their everyday needs. Vera is now bedridden, but she remains an important part of the story and heart of RUKAVYCHKA.

They do not do this for recognition. They do it because these animals depend on them.

A day at RUKAVYCHKA

A shelter is not just a place. It is a thousand small acts of care, repeated every single day.

Preparing meals Large pots of porridge and food prepared for hungry dogs.
Cleaning and care Keeping living spaces clean, checking on every dog and making sure they are comfortable.
Health and treatment Vaccinations, parasite prevention, medicines and attention when a dog needs help.
Love and attention A kind voice, a gentle touch, a game or simply someone sitting nearby.

When the war changed everything

The war brought new dangers and enormous challenges. Shelling damaged parts of the shelter, and the dogs became vulnerable to frightening sounds, cold weather and uncertainty.

Repairs, heating, food and everyday care became even harder to manage. Yet Valentin continued doing what he could for the animals who depended on him.

Today, RUKAVYCHKA still needs support to keep the dogs safe, warm, fed and healthy.

More than survival

RUKAVYCHKA is not only about difficult days. It is also about wagging tails, playful moments, familiar faces and dogs learning to trust again.

Some have already found loving families. Others are still waiting. Every adoption, every bag of food and every helping hand makes a real difference.

We want every dog to have the chance to experience a safe and loving home.

A separate safe place

Alongside the main shelter, there is also a separate volunteer foster home that gives some dogs — including dogs rescued from military-affected areas — a temporary place of safety and care. It is a different home, but part of the same wider effort to help dogs in need.

Be part of their story

You do not have to do something extraordinary. Adopting, donating, sharing our story or sending supplies can change a dog's life.
